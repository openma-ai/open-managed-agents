# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and technical teams who operate self-hosted or managed AI agents through the OpenMA API, Console, and CLI. They configure agents and environments, inspect durable sessions, follow tool activity, and need the same runtime truth to remain legible across graphical and terminal surfaces.

## Product Purpose

Open Managed Agents is an open-source agent platform and self-hosted managed-agents API. It provides durable sessions, sandboxed execution, tools, memory, vaults, integrations, observability, and crash recovery behind one coherent Console and CLI. Success means users can deploy and operate agents confidently while every surface reflects the canonical event log instead of inventing its own interpretation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and responsive. The product should feel technically capable without becoming noisy, ornamental, or self-important. Copy is direct and concrete; state changes are immediate and spatially stable.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI dashboards with fabricated capability labels or decorative model chrome.
- Console and CLI views that disagree about message, tool, error, or turn state.
- Dense streams of raw events when a compact canonical conversation is more useful.
- Heavy glass effects, decorative animation, and low-contrast inactive states.
- Layouts that jump, squeeze, or expose accidental scrollbars as live events arrive.

## Design Principles

- Show live truth from the canonical event log.
- Normalize once, project everywhere: Console, Web, and CLI share contracts instead of duplicating event rules.
- Preserve spatial continuity while streams become committed events.
- Use progressive disclosure for tool details, reasoning, and observability data.
- Prefer concrete action language and accessible status cues.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, semantic labels, WCAG AA text contrast in all themes, and reduced-motion fallbacks. Color must never be the only carrier of state, and terminal output must remain readable without ANSI color.
